03 · projects

  
Things I've shipped

  

    

      
live_spectrogram.render()

      

        
2026 · AUDIO · ALGORITHMS

        
Mini Shazam 🎵

        
A Shazam-style audio fingerprinting app built from scratch in Python, with no ML libraries. It identifies a song from a short, noisy 3–10 s clip using spectrogram peak detection, landmark hashing and offset-histogram voting over 999 tracks from the GTZAN dataset. Live on Streamlit with upload, mic recording and match visualisations, plus pytest tests.

        

          
94–96%clean accuracy

          
92%5 s clip @ 10 dB noise

          
46.7 MBfor 12.8M hashes

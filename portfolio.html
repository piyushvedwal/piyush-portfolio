Audio fingerprinting
01 — Mini Shazam2026

Recognising songs from a noisy three-second clip.

A Shazam-style recognition engine written from scratch in Python, with no ML libraries. It uses spectrogram peak detection, landmark hashing and offset-histogram voting to search 999 tracks from the GTZAN dataset. Shipped as a live Streamlit app with upload, mic recording, match visualisations and a pytest suite.

Privacy-preserving ML
02 — Synthetic Data Generator2026

Realistic data that can't leak who was in it.

Generates synthetic tabular data with a formal differential privacy guarantee: a GAN whose discriminator is trained with DP-SGD through Opacus, so every synthetic row inherits the (ε, δ) budget. Privacy is then tested empirically with a membership inference attack on held-out rows and a distance-to-closest-record check, with fidelity and utility metrics exported as a report. Built with Ganesh Khetawat.
